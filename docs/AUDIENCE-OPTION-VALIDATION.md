# SBNS Audience & Option Validation Operating Plan

Status: current operating plan
Validation phase: October–December 2026
Longer planning horizon: October 2026–September 2027
Purpose: grow evidence about audience and product value without turning SBNS into a content treadmill, marketing job, or speculative technology project.

## Governing premise

SBNS should earn the right to scale.

During validation, the publication should learn whether strong accountability reporting, distinctive evidence presentation, durable discovery, and a direct reader relationship can create repeat use and authority at a founder burden that remains sustainable.

Publication has no cadence requirement.

Measurement has a cadence.

## Operating modes

Use one mode at a time:

- **Maintenance** — protect continuity, fix a real issue, avoid expansion.
- **Normal** — ordinary low-burden editorial and measurement work.
- **Active Story** — one meaningful candidate deserves focused research or production.
- **Surge** — a genuinely time-sensitive story or operational event temporarily justifies higher effort.
- **Recovery** — reduce load after a surge, incident, or high-burden production cycle.
- **Build** — bounded implementation required to unlock or protect meaningful value.
- **Dormant** — intentionally leave SBNS alone when no action is worth the founder burden.

If evidence is insufficient, default to Normal rather than inventing urgency.

## Priority order

Evaluate work in this order:

1. protect editorial quality, site health, reader trust, or an active high-value story;
2. complete the 90-day Audience & Option Validation work: measurement, Search Console/indexing, story-level analytics, share/copy measurement, direct-reader capture, scorecard, and founder-burden tracking;
3. strengthen owned audience and durable discovery: search, internal linking, permanent story URLs, social-preview/share assets, direct-reader signup, and selective earned outreach;
4. run only bounded growth experiments with a hypothesis, measurement, time/cash cap, and stopping rule;
5. improve transferability or reduce recurring founder labor through documentation or automation;
6. consider revenue, paid acquisition, advanced tooling, or expansion only when prior evidence and spending gates justify it.

## 90-day validation questions

The October–December phase should establish decision-useful evidence about:

- Reach — are real readers finding SBNS?
- Loyalty — do any readers return?
- Depth — do readers engage beyond a superficial visit?
- Sharing — are stories shared or copied?
- Discovery — what comes through search, referrals, or direct navigation?
- Direct Relationship — will readers choose a direct SBNS connection?
- Authority — does reporting earn citations, responses, useful inbound interest, or durable references?
- Editorial Quality — does the process preserve evidence, qualification, chronology, and causation boundaries?
- Founder Burden — how much time and cognitive load does each useful result require?
- Economics — what recurring and one-time costs are actually needed?
- Asset Growth — does each cycle leave behind reusable reporting, audience, documentation, source knowledge, or production capability?

No metric should be guessed when instrumentation is absent.

## Measurement sequence

Preferred order:

1. Cloudflare Web Analytics and reliable baseline traffic measurement;
2. story-level measurement;
3. referral, Share, and Copy Link behavior;
4. search-indexing and Search Console evidence;
5. direct-reader capture;
6. bounded distribution experiments after organic evidence exists.

Do not block worthwhile reporting merely because every measurement layer is incomplete.

## Founder-burden and cost posture

Normal operating target:

- roughly 2–4 founder hours per week;
- prefer one 15–45 minute useful action over a long checklist;
- treat attention and context switching as real costs.

Cost posture:

- target $0 new recurring software spend during validation;
- soft ceiling $25/month only when evidence justifies it;
- $0 paid acquisition until measurement plus direct-reader conversion are working;
- no premium SEO, analytics, newsletter, ad, PR, image, or social tool without a specific validated bottleneck.

A paid tool is justified only when it credibly removes more recurring burden than it adds.

## Distribution boundaries

SBNS does not require a social-media posting machine.

Do not:

- recommend LinkedIn as a distribution route;
- create a posting obligation merely to feed an algorithm;
- publish weak material for consistency;
- manufacture partnerships or outreach volume;
- treat follower count as a substitute for reader value.

Prefer:

- permanent story URLs;
- search;
- RSS/follow;
- reader-initiated sharing;
- direct-reader relationships;
- selective earned outreach when a specific story justifies it.

## Growth-experiment contract

Every experiment needs:

- one hypothesis;
- one observable success signal;
- a time cap;
- a cash cap;
- a stopping rule.

Examples may include a search/title test, a direct-reader signup placement, a story-specific earned outreach test, or an internal-linking pattern.

Do not run experiments merely because a channel exists.

## Hard stop rules

Stop when:

- added work is unlikely to change a decision;
- measurement cannot distinguish success from noise;
- the experiment requires recurring labor disproportionate to likely value;
- the publication would need weaker content to sustain the tactic;
- the tactic creates an algorithmic posting obligation;
- a tool adds more maintenance than it removes;
- cost crosses the soft ceiling without evidence;
- a proposed growth feature is not tied to an observed bottleneck.

"No action required today" is a successful operating result.

## Decision gates beyond validation

Do not advance to paid acquisition, heavier revenue work, advanced tooling, or expansion merely because October–December ends.

Advancement requires evidence that:

- worthy stories can be produced at sustainable burden;
- measurement is reliable enough to support decisions;
- at least some durable audience/discovery path is functioning;
- direct-reader conversion can be measured before paid acquisition;
- incremental cost has a plausible return in audience, authority, burden reduction, or asset value.

The goal is option value, not premature scale.
