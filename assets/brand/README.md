# Canonical share-card brand inputs

`brand-tokens.json` and `sbns-social-og.svg` are unchanged copies from
`SBNS_Brand_Guide_Full_Package_v1.0.zip`, adopted on 20 September 2026 with
`SBNS_Brand_and_Design_Guide_v1.0.md` as the canonical visual authority.

`sbns-mark.svg` is the bounded browser/reader extraction of the approved
document, magnifying-glass, and centered-star geometry from
`sbns-social-og.svg`. The public favicon and masthead mark are byte-identical
copies of this source asset; no alternate lettermark or newspaper icon is used.

The deterministic story-card generator directly reuses the canonical social
template through its header divider: dark field, approved page/lens/star mark,
wordmark, descriptor, and spacing remain the master composition. The static
campaign message below the divider is replaced with story category, date,
headline, neutral share-card severity indicator, and canonical domain. The divider is
normalized to the canonical Newsprint Gray token.

`The Institutional Failure Desk` is intentionally omitted from this surface.
The canonical descriptor already occupies the single supporting-message slot,
and the story headline must outrank additional brand language.

The public reader uses Signal Red only for the filled portion of its ordinal
severity meter; empty dots remain neutral and the numeric value plus accessible
label remain authoritative. This is a bounded reader convention, not a claim
about verification confidence, guilt, factual certainty, or partisan meaning.
